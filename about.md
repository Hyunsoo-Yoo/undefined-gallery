# About

Hello, this is Leo.

Welcome to the first introduction of Undefined Gallery.  
To everyone reading this, it’s a pleasure to meet you.

I love both engineering and the humanities — philosophy and art.  
Though they may seem far apart, I believe they ultimately converge into a single question:

“What problem should we define, and how should we think about it as human beings?”

The speed of AI development has already surpassed imagination.  
Productivity and convenience are rising exponentially,  
and technology will undoubtedly make the future more abundant.

But not everyone will be equally nourished.

The society ahead will no longer reward those who are simply “smart.”  
It will reward those who can define meaningful problems.

More than Makers who build quickly,  
the world will need Designers who determine what should be built in the first place.

More fundamentally,  
the future will be shaped by those who can discover real problems in society,  
understand them deeply,  
and strive to solve them.

At the origin of all of this lies something uniquely human: creativity.

Knowing how to use AI tools is no longer a differentiator.  
It will become basic literacy.

The true gap will emerge from how deeply one can think.

In this age of competition,  
we must cultivate the habit of deep thinking.

Question what you take for granted.

University systems.  
Conventional formulas for success.  
Ideas that have solidified into “obvious truths.”

Will they still remain valid?

Our most powerful weapon now  
is deep thinking and creative ideas.

I created Undefined Gallery to sharpen that weapon.

To think more deeply.  
To think more creatively.

We must step beyond familiar environments  
and continually confront new perspectives.

And at the center of it all, there are always people.

Let’s engage in deep, honest, and creative conversations.

Deep Thinking and Creativity cannot be clearly defined.  
That is why I call this space Undefined.

Because it cannot be defined,  
it requires experience, experimentation, and continuous exploration.

Undefined Gallery is an laboratory
where we grow Deep Thinking, creativity, and vision together.

If you would like to join us,  
send me a DM.

I look forward to connecting.
